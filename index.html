About the Creator

Yuvraj Gopalbhai Barot

I’m Yuvraj Gopalbhai Barot, a student of artificial intelligence and data science who enjoys creating things that connect technology with the real world. GreenMetal AI is my exploration into how intelligent models can help restore damaged land using hyperaccumulator plants. The project simulates soil conditions, predicts metal recovery potential, and makes the science behind phytoremediation easier to understand. You can see more of my work or reach me through GitHub or LinkedIn.
